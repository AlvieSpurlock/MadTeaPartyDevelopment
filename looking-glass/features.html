Features

Everything Looking Glass does

Every item here is in the app today.

Voice-triggered clipping

Say it on stream, and it's a clip.

  • Finds every “clip this” and “clip that”
  • Add your own clip words
  • Set your own clip lengths
  • Works from a single recording or a whole VOD

Smart crop

Made for phones without you lifting a finger.

  • Vertical 9:16 by default
  • Put Splitscreen Left, Right, Top or Bottom in the title and it crops that part

Written for every clip

Each clip gets its own words.

  • Captions and silence cuts
  • Music (only use music you have the rights to)
  • Titles, descriptions, hashtags and search tags

Raw copies too

For when you want to put your own touch on it.

  • Every clip keeps a raw copy
  • Upload your polished version and it replaces the generated one before it posts

Going live and weekly recaps

  • Going-live posts from your own notification text
  • Going-live detection for Twitch
  • A weekly compilation for YouTube

Posting and analytics

  • X, Bluesky, Threads, Instagram, Facebook, Mastodon and Tumblr
  • Schedules at the best posting times
  • Analytics for what's working
  • TikTok: a ready-to-post doc, or auto-post with your own TikTok developer sign-in
Good to know: auto-posting uses your own free developer apps on each platform. Optional Twitch downloading is off by default because it may break Twitch's terms. Only use music you have the rights to.
