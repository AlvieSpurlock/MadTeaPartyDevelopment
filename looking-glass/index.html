Why I Built It

Honestly? So I could keep making games and still be home for dinner.

Mad Tea Party is one person. Marketing well is a full-time job on its own, and I couldn't do it, keep building games and software, provide for my family, and still have time to be with them. So I built Looking Glass to carry the clipping and posting while I build.

It's a stand-in, not a replacement. As soon as I can afford to, I'll hire real people for these roles, and the day a role is filled, I stop using Looking Glass for that job in my own studio.

Roles I plan to hire for

  • Video and clip editor
  • Social media manager
  • Community manager
  • Social copywriter (titles, captions and descriptions)

Questions

Is it a subscription?

No. One payment, yours to keep.

Does it post on its own?

Only what you approved, or everything once you turn on automatic approval. Going-live posts use your own words.

Does every clip go everywhere?

Yes, by default: one approval posts it on every platform you've signed in to, each at its own pace. You can leave a platform out.

What if I didn't mean to say “clip that”?

Every clip word is checked by listening again; silence, music and repeated loops don't make clips. Remove any clip with one click.
