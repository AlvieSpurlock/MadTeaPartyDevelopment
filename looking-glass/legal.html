Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026.

The app

  • Looking Glass keeps your data (projects, contacts, drafts, clips, settings and history) in its folder on your PC. Nothing is uploaded to me.
  • The AI runs on your PC through Ollama. Your data is not sent to an AI company.
  • The app only contacts the services you connect or turn on yourself: the social platforms you sign in to, your email account, web search, and, only if you turn on “Open it from anywhere”, Cloudflare's secure link. What those services do with data is covered by their own privacy policies.

Buying

Checkout is handled by Gumroad, which collects what a purchase needs (such as your email address and payment details) under its own privacy policy. I receive your email address and order details so I can deliver the app and help with support. I never sell or share them.

This website

The site counts visits to its main pages: it records only the page name and how the visitor arrived (for example “direct” or a link's source), with no cookies and no visitor ID, in a Google Sheet. Visitors with Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control turned on are not counted. Fonts load from Google Fonts.
