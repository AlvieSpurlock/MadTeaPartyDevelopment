Setup Guide Full guide at launch

What it walks you through:

  1. Running the installer: double-click Run Looking Glass.
  2. What installs on first run (Python, ffmpeg, Ollama and its AI models, the speech-to-text model, OpenCV and free fonts), all free.
  3. Pointing it at your stream recordings (OBS or your platform's download).
  4. Creating your free developer sign-in on each platform for auto-posting, one by one.
  5. Phone access: home Wi-Fi, or a secure link with a passcode (optional free Cloudflare account for a fixed link).
  6. Optional: turning on automatic Twitch downloading (off by default; may break Twitch's terms).

Operating Guide Full guide at launch

What it covers:

  1. The questionnaire: your channel, platforms and voice.
  2. Clip words: the built-in ones, your own words and lengths, and Second Looks.
  3. The Studio and the Edit Room: finishing, fixing captions, stitching moments, your stash of media and music.
  4. Approving: one approval for every platform, or automatic approval.
  5. Grow: pacing, the Drop Calendar and Mass reschedule, going-live posts, Best Of and Growth.
  6. Channels: running several at once, each with its own settings.
  7. Updating: download the newest version from Gumroad; your settings and data stay.
