The Madness

Games aren't just code and pixels. They're homes.

The heart of it all

Games are the places we run to when the world feels too loud or too quiet. Here, connection comes before commerce. Whimsy is free. And everyone who steps inside gets a seat at the table, no invitation required.

Meet Alvie, the Mad Hatter himself

Alvie grew up wandering empty hallways until heroes like Sora and Riku showed up on screen, offering friendship when real life didn't. A dusty PlayStation 2 became his escape hatch, his compass, and eventually his calling.

His wife crowned him "Mad Hatter," a name born from love, chaos, and 3 AM ideas scribbled on napkins. So when it came time to name his studio, he reached for the Hatter's oldest partner in madness: the March Hare, forever pouring tea and forever wide awake. March Hare Games is a love letter to his wife, his kids, and every dreamer who ever needed a world to belong to.
