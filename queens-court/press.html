Short description

Queens Court finds the people who should hear about your game, product or business, writes every pitch, post and follow-up in your voice, clips your streams, and waits for your yes before anything goes out.

Long description

Queens Court is a Windows app that does the promotion work of a small marketing team on your own PC. It finds press, creators, blogs, podcasts, organizations and communities; writes pitches, follow-ups and replies in your voice, learning from every approve, edit and reject; answers people's questions, and saves the ones it can't answer so it remembers your reply; posts to seven social platforms at a pace that grows with you; turns stream recordings into finished vertical clips with a Clip Editor that learns your editing style; and finds grants and awards and drafts your answers. Nothing is sent until you approve it, on your PC or from your phone. The AI runs on your own PC. Mad Tea Party Development uses it to market its games and apps, and plans to hand that work to human hires as soon as it can afford to.

Key features

  • Finds press, creators, blogs, podcasts, organizations and communities
  • Writes in your voice, and learns from every approve, edit and reject
  • Replies that answer questions, with a QnA Memory for the ones only you can answer
  • Emails, contact forms, DMs, call scripts and texts in one place
  • Posts on X, Bluesky, Threads, Instagram, Facebook, Mastodon and Tumblr, paced to grow
  • Stream clips: voice clip words, a Studio that finishes each clip, and a Clip Editor that learns your style
  • Finds grants and awards, breaks them down and drafts your answers
  • Swipe to approve from your phone; nothing goes out until you say yes
