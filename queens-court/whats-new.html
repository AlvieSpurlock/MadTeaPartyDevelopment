What's new in Queens Court

The latest improvements. Everything here is in the app today, and in every copy you can download from your Gumroad library.

  • Sort first, in your words: Every clip waits for you: Fun or Educational, and one or two sentences of what happens. Titles are built on your words; descriptions are built off the title for search.
  • Hooks that stop the scroll: One hook per proven structure (curiosity gap, open loop, stakes, contrarian, warning, relatable pain), best first for the kind of clip, and an optional cold open that shows the payoff first.
  • Thumbnails: Made from the big moment with 2-4 big words, yours to change, and uploaded to YouTube with the clip.
  • Stream types: Gaming, Software, Art and Just talking, each with its own built-in editing, plus Fun and Educational on top.
  • Automatic editing for retention: Clips start on the first word, never on dead air; pauses and filler words are cut by the words themselves in talking clips; a long build-up opens on its payoff.
  • A base style for everyone: Learned from real Shorts in every genre, so new clips look right from the first stream.
  • Daily Top 5 / Top 10: A busy day's best moments, counted down in one Short.
  • One stream, several titles: Change the title when you change games; each part is clipped as its own game.
  • Each game gets its share: Your clips a day split between this week's games, evenly or by views.
  • Edit Room upgrades: Music and sounds play on the timeline, the real render plays full size under it, a transcript table, a wobble switch per sticker, a crop per part, and another clip from the same moment.
  • Clean sound: No more crackle or drift: lossless between steps, one final encode, a limiter, cuts on frame edges.
  • Reddit posts and Instagram screenshot posts: Written for each subreddit from its rules and top posts; screenshot posts with your own prompt.
  • Your comment on every video: Including the ones you made yourself.
  • Stream titles with emoji clip like any other.
  • A free Queens Court demo: Find outlets that fit your game and read the pitches it writes, before you buy. Nothing is ever sent from the demo.
  • Every project's posts in one place: Approve every project's posts together, with media add, change and remove, and fresh hashtags on each card.
  • Friendlier emails: Pitches end with a short, human sign-off instead of a boilerplate footer.
  • A social-only project: Gets the Looking Glass look and menu inside Queens Court.
  • Post times that learn from your results: Every post and clip is scored against your usual, and new posts go out at the hours that work best for you, per platform. No screenshots needed.
  • Analytics read by themselves: YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, Threads and TikTok through their own APIs (views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves, watch time), plus Bluesky, Mastodon and Tumblr.
  • Editing that follows your YouTube numbers: New clips are cut where your best clips hold viewers, with a note in the editor saying why. Pick longer versions three times in a row and it stops shortening.
  • Full YouTube details: Translated titles, descriptions and caption tracks, a playlist per game, made-for-kids and paid-promotion labels, recording date and brand partner.
  • Instagram Reels done right: Shown in the main feed, the cover set to the clip's big moment, collaborators and a place tag.
  • Your comment on every clip: Posted for you on YouTube, Facebook and Instagram once the clip is live.
  • Smarter pacing: Starts at 3 clips a day on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook; posts spread through the day, never bunched; one shared schedule across all your projects so nothing collides.
  • A higher YouTube ceiling: With an automatic pause if YouTube says the channel has hit its daily upload limit.
  • Per-project parts: Switch each part of a project on or off (with presets). Parts you switch off fold away and clear their leftovers at your next sign-off.
  • Stream routing: Skip streams by words in the title, or have every project leave the other projects' streams alone. Matching ignores capitals and catches typos.
  • Training that keeps up: While you're signed off, it makes practice cards for every project; each kind of writing stops practicing once it's trained; new projects get practice cards even before you add facts.
  • Keep posting even without news: A per-project option for steady, evergreen marketing: topics come back on their own schedule, always in fresh words.
  • Going-live posts on their own: Keep going-live posts on even when social posts are off.
  • Never lose a setting: A “Not saved yet” note lists every change you haven't saved.
  • New in-depth guides: The Setup Guide and the Operating Guide as PDFs, with contents pages and bookmarks.
  • No street addresses: Posts never give a street address, phone number or opening hours.
