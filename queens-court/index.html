Why I Built It

Honestly? So I could keep making games and still be home for dinner.

Mad Tea Party is one person. Marketing well is a full-time job on its own, and I couldn't do it, keep building games and software, provide for my family, and still have time to be with them. So I built Queens Court to carry the outreach while I build.

It's a stand-in, not a replacement. As soon as I can afford to, I'll hire real people for these roles, and the day a role is filled, I stop using Queens Court for that job in my own studio.

Roles I plan to hire for

  • PR and press outreach coordinator
  • Community manager
  • Social media manager
  • Marketing copywriter
  • Creator and influencer partnerships coordinator
  • Video and clip editor
  • Grant writer

What that means for you

  • Your copy is yours to keep and use however you like. This promise is about how I run my own studio.
  • Every sale helps bring those hires closer.
  • Nothing goes out without a human saying yes, in my studio or yours.

Questions

Is it a subscription?

No. One payment, yours to keep.

Does it send things on its own?

Only what you approved. Emails go out at a safe pace; posts and clips go out at their scheduled times.

Which AI does it use?

Free open models that run on your PC through Ollama. Nothing is sent to an AI company, and there's no AI bill.

Can I use it from my phone?

Yes: on your home Wi-Fi, or from anywhere with a secure link and a passcode.
