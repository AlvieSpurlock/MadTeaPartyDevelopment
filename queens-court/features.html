Everything Queens Court does

Grouped the way the app's menu is, so you'll recognize it the day you open it. Every item here is in the app today.

Daily

One place to start your day.

  • Today: one checklist for the day
  • Swipe: approve, edit or reject from your phone
  • Approved: everything you said yes to

Outreach

The people who should hear about your work, and every message to them.

  • Finds press, creators, blogs, podcasts, organizations and communities
  • Pitches, follow-ups and replies in your voice
  • Handle Emails, Forms, DMs, Calls and Texts
  • Replies that answer questions; when it can't answer, it saves the question for you and remembers your answer (QnA Memory)

Find people and Awards

  • Outreach Locator and Social Avenues
  • Coverage: finds coverage of you online
  • Grants (money) and Awards (recognition): found, broken down, answers drafted

Posts

  • Update posts on X, Bluesky, Threads, Instagram, Facebook, Mastodon and Tumblr
  • Hashtags chosen for reach
  • Posts a day start at a sensible number and grow when you keep up and your followers grow
  • Topics come back with a new angle

Clips (when you stream)

Turn it on in the questionnaire.

  • Say “clip this” (the moment after) or “clip that” (the moment before), or your own clip words with their own lengths; a word said right after one follows its direction
  • Every clip word is double-checked by listening again, so silence or music never makes a clip
  • Each clip is scored; doubtful ones wait for a second look
  • The Studio finishes each clip: smart 9:16 crop or a facecam layout, even sound, zoom punches, effects, color grade, the moment's sound and GIF, word stickers, captions in your chosen style, background music with its credit line in the description
  • The Clip Editor: approve as made, change anything and approve your version, or build one from scratch; fix what the captions say; stitch moments together with transitions (fade, wipe, slide, zoom and more); add your own images, GIFs, video clips and sounds
  • After 30 approved versions it makes clips in your style
  • Titles, descriptions and hashtags written from the game, the moment and what was said, with the game's own hashtags added
  • Every clip on every platform you've signed in to, with one approval; each platform at its own clips a day (starts low, goes up after 3 days in a row while your followers aren't dropping); Mass reschedule fixes a crowded schedule
  • Clips your older streams too, one at a time, and keeps a record so none is clipped twice
  • A plain-language progress card: which stream, which step, how many clips are made

Projects

  • One project per game, product or channel, each with its own settings, posts, clips and history
  • A gaming-channel type for clips and going-live posts only

Your phone

  • Open it on your home Wi-Fi
  • Or from anywhere through a secure link with a passcode

Learning

  • Lessons, Training and Working Knowledge
  • The writer learns your taste and only uses facts you've confirmed
Good to know: auto-posting uses your own free developer sign-ins on each platform (the Setup Guide walks through each). Optional Twitch downloading is off by default because it may break Twitch's terms. Only use music and media you have the rights to; credit lines are added for you.
