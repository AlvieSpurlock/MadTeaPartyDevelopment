The Legend

You are the Hero of Toadstool, a beautiful town… or at least it was the last time you saw it. This time you enter the town in ruins, and the only survivor is Princess Winterfall, off in the distance.

She tells you to go check on the usual suspect. Y'know, the usual "keep going straight into the cave of wonders and take a left" schtick?

Well, when you get there, things aren't right at all. You have become a ball. Not just that, you're in a strange version of the Castle you've fought through so many times before.

The ONLY. HOPE. of escaping this place is to explore every nook and cranny. Maybe you'll find something…

Warning: may cause rage
