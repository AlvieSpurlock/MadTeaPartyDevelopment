What It Does

A full-fidelity combat probability simulator for D&D-style tabletop RPGs. Instead of guessing from vague CR guidelines, it plays the fight out for real, thousands of times.

⚔️

Models Every Combatant

Class features, feats, spell slots, gear and monster stat blocks, pre-built or custom.

🗺️

Fights on a Grid

Tracks positioning, range, movement and attacks of opportunity on a 2D grid.

🔥

Resolves Everything

Attacks, spells, AoE, DoT, conditions, resistances, vulnerabilities and immunities, all by 5E rules.

🧬

Clone in a Click

Duplicate any combatant to build groups, swarms or reinforcement waves instantly.

How It Works

Define the combatants

  • Party stats (27-point buy or 4d6)
  • Class features, feats and gear
  • Pre-built or custom monsters
  • Class-based or user-defined behavior

Run 150,000 battles

  • Roll initiative and use 5E action economy
  • Move, attack, cast and trigger opportunity attacks
  • Track spell slots, focus and targeting
  • Fight until one side wins

Read the verdict

  • Overall encounter winnability
  • Compare encounters side by side
  • Tweak variables and re-run
  • Iterate until the fight feels right

Built for the table

A full wxWidgets interface with your party on one side and the enemies on the other. Set it up, hit run, and watch the probabilities roll in.

  • Monte Carlo, the same technique used in finance, physics and AI
  • Class-based logic and user-defined AI
  • AC capped at 18 to keep 5E balance

Pixel-art battles

Sound effects, pixel-art VFX, a block-based 2D combat renderer and background music make testing intuitive and fun.

  • None of it changes the math, which stays fully accurate
  • Watch the fight happen, not just the numbers
