Why Asset Logger?

Steam requires clear documentation for every third-party asset in your game. Tracking that in spreadsheets is slow, error-prone and easy to forget.

The spreadsheet way

  • Re-typing the same fields again and again
  • Inconsistent formatting
  • Duplicate or missing entries
  • Typos you won't notice until review

The Asset Logger way

  • Prompts you for every required field
  • Consistent formatting, every time
  • Confirms each asset before saving
  • Clean CSV, ready to drop into your submission

Perfect for

Indie devs preparing Steam submissions
Teams tracking assets across levels or scenes
Solo devs who want clean docs without spreadsheets
Anyone who needs a simple, repeatable workflow
