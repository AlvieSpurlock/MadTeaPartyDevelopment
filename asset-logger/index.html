Is it a subscription?

No. One payment, yours to keep.

What does the CSV contain?

One row per asset: Platform, Location (in game), Use (in game), Asset Name, Asset Maker and License Type.

Can I log lots of assets at once?

Yes. After each one it asks "Do you have more assets?" and keeps going until you're done.

Will it overwrite my old file?

Only if you say so. If the CSV already exists, it warns you and asks first.
