Windows 10 or 11 · Works offline · Editable files included

Paid installs rarely pay for themselves in the first month.
Whether they ever do depends on retention, revenue per user, what the network and store keep, how much a big wave of installs costs, and how much of your earnings you put back in.
That's a lot of moving parts for a spreadsheet, and more so with several games.

What It Does

📉

Cohorts and retention

Every install wave becomes a cohort that decays along an eight-point retention curve you set (days 1, 7, 30, 60, 90, 120, 160, 180).

💸

Free and paid installs

Set free installs and a paid budget, then reinvest a percentage of revenue into more paid installs.

🎯

Paid channels

Split the paid budget across up to three markets, each with its own cost per install and its own retention scale.

🛒

Ads, purchases and subscriptions

Ad revenue per user, in-app purchase revenue per user, and subscriptions, each with the right cut taken out.

🧾

Real-world costs

Revenue cut, running costs, one-time dev cost, studio overhead, ad-network warm-up, ARPDAU drift, seasonality, CPI that rises with spend, and tax with or without loss carry-forward.

🗓️

Many games, one timeline

Stagger launch days and cross-promote between games, with a weight and a cap per game, and optional chaining.

🎲

Range analysis

Re-run the whole portfolio 200 times with your inputs nudged within spreads you set, and see the low, middle and high outcomes.

📊

Plain results

DAU, revenue against costs, breakeven day, return on spend, monthly revenue and take-home profit, in eight currencies.

Questions

Does it need the internet?

No. The charts library is bundled and the app runs offline. The one exception is an optional Fetch current rates button for exchange rates, which contacts an exchange-rate service only when you press it.

How accurate is it?

The math is exact and you can check it by hand. Forecasts depend on your assumptions and have not been validated against live games. The starting values are rough estimates. The range analysis shows how far the result moves if your inputs are off by amounts you choose. See Accuracy and limits.

What does the range analysis tell me?

It re-runs your portfolio 200 times with four inputs (retention, revenue per user, CPI and free installs) nudged within the spreads you set, and shows the low, middle and high results. It measures how sensitive your plan is to those guesses. It is not a probability of success in the real world.

Can I save my scenarios?

Not in version 1.0, and there's no export yet; closing the app clears your inputs. You can set your own starting values in config.js so new games open with your base case.

Can I resell it, share it, or run it for my clients?

No. The license covers one business, for its own internal use. Each client business needs its own license. Selling, sharing, publishing or building a competing product from it is not allowed. See the license.
