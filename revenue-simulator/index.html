▮▮▮ Mobile Game Revenue Simulator · for Windows

Model your mobile game's players and ad revenue before you spend on UA.

A Windows desktop simulator for retention, free and paid installs, reinvestment and multi-game portfolios. You set the assumptions; it shows what they add up to.

Get it See how it works

Windows 10 or 11 · Works offline · Editable files included

Paid installs rarely pay for themselves in the first month.
Whether they ever do depends on retention, ad revenue per user, what the network and platform keep, how much a big wave of installs costs, and how much you put back in.
That's a lot of moving parts for a spreadsheet, and more so with several games.

What It Does

📉

Cohorts and retention

Every install wave becomes a cohort that decays along an eight-point retention curve you set (days 1, 7, 30, 60, 90, 120, 160, 180).

💸

Free and paid installs

Set free installs and a paid budget at a market CPI, then reinvest a percentage of revenue into more paid installs.

🎯

Paid users that retain differently

Give bought installs their own retention curve.

🧾

Real-world costs

Revenue cut, running costs, one-time dev cost, ad-network warm-up, ARPDAU drift, seasonality, CPI that rises with spend, and a flat tax estimate.

🗓️

Many games, one timeline

Stagger launch days, cross-promote between games, and optionally chain the cross-promo.

📊

Plain results

DAU, revenue against costs, breakeven day, return on spend, monthly revenue and monthly take-home profit, in eight currencies.

Questions

Does it need the internet?

No. The charts library is bundled and the app's own code makes no network requests.

Why does Windows warn me when I open it?

The app isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen shows a warning on first launch. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

How accurate is it?

The math is exact and you can check it by hand. Forecasts depend on your assumptions and have not been validated against live games. The starting values are rough estimates. See Accuracy and limits.

Can I save my scenarios?

Not in version 1.0, and there's no export yet; closing the app clears your inputs. You can set your own starting values in config.js so new games open with your base case.

Can I resell it, share it, or run it for my clients?

No. The license covers one business, for its own internal use. Each client business needs its own license. Selling, sharing, publishing or building a competing product from it is not allowed. See the license.
