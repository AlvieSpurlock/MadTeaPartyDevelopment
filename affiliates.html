Become an Affiliate

Share Looking Glass, Queens Court or the Mobile Game Revenue Simulator with your audience and keep 25% of every sale you send. Any audience size welcome.

What you get

  • 25% of every sale through your link, paid automatically by Gumroad.
  • A 30%-off code for your viewers, with your name on it.
  • The free demo, so you can try it and show it on your own stream.
  • Ready-made assets: clips, screenshots, a one-line pitch and a chat-command line.
  • A free copy once your sales cover the price, on top of your 25%.
  • A weekly check-in your first month, with a fresh clip or post idea and anything new in the apps.
  • A day-1 welcome kit: one post to make today, a chat command, a panel image and a tips PDF, with your link within 24 hours.
  • The affiliates' Discord: share wins, swap clip ideas and join the monthly raid train, where affiliates raid each other's streams.
  • Bring a friend: refer 3 streamers who join and each make a sale, and Queens Court is yours free, with a badge on the leaderboard.
  • A monthly note after that with a fresh limited-time code for your viewers, the month's top 5 sellers and a shout-out for every affiliate's first sale. You're only named if you say yes.

How it works

  1. Fill in the 5-question form: your name, email, channel, who sent you (if anyone), and whether you'd like to be named in shout-outs. You don't have to use the apps to share them, but every affiliate gets the free demo to try.
  2. Within 24 hours I email you your affiliate link, your viewer code, the demo, the assets and your day-1 kit.
  3. Show it on stream, in panels, on clips or in videos, with your link.
  4. Every sale through your link pays you 25%.

The fine print

  • Only share your own experience. If you haven't used it, share it as a recommendation (“check out this tool”), not as “I use this every day”. Claiming to use something you don't breaks the FTC's endorsement rules.
  • Always disclose it. Say you earn from the link (“affiliate link” or #ad). The FTC requires it, and your viewers trust it more.
  • No spam. Share it where it fits; no mass posting, fake reviews or posing as an unrelated customer.
  • Shout-outs are opt-in. You're only named on the leaderboard or in the monthly note if you say yes, and dollar amounts are never shared without your OK.
  • Your own license only. One license covers one person or business and their own projects; running it for clients needs a license for each business.
