Your Dragon

Scorchlet is a cute little dragon that you get to watch grow. You watch it play, sleep, and eat, and it gives you love every chance it gets.

🥚

Watch It Grow

From hatchling to high-flyer. Watch your dragon play, nap and munch its way through each day.

💞

Bond Your Way

Feed it and watch it sleep, or go the Treats & Tickle route for a bonus on your next minigame.

⛏️

Earn & Upgrade

Mine for money to buy toys, treats, snacks and upgrades to the food bowl.

🍖

Hunt for Food

Head out hunting to gather food and keep your little critter's belly full.

Bonding

There are lots of ways to bond with Scorchlet. Keep it simple by feeding and watching it sleep, or get hands-on with treats and tickles.

  • Core: Feed & Sleep
  • Treat: Snack & Sweet
  • Tickle: 8 different animations to discover
  • Treats & Tickles earn a bonus on your next minigame (except Hunting). It resets when Home loads again.

One of a Kind

Every new save rolls a brand-new dragon. Its body (pool of 4), color (5 per body) and eyes (5 per body) are all randomized. Then come its tastes.

LikedHappy animation, hearts pop above
DislikedSad, angry or scared, with purple smoke

There are 10 snacks, and each dragon loves or hates them differently. Learn what yours likes.

Flappy Dragon

Mining

  • Night-time only
  • Funds every treat and toy

Built for Everyone

Scorchlet was built so every traveler can join the journey, no matter how they play.

🎨

Color-blind Friendly

Clear shapes and strong contrast throughout the UI.

🎮

Simple Controls

No more than 3 buttons or a single joystick combo, with full controller support.

🔇

Playable Without Audio

No game-changing sound cues, and never more than three sounds at once.

🧮

Gentle Math

Minimal text and numbers, with upgrades designed for young and neurodivergent players.

🤲

Built with Tremors in Mind

Large, standout core buttons for comfort and clarity.

🔉

Balanced Stimulation

One strong sound, up to two soft ones. Calm, never overwhelming.
