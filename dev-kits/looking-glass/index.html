Why I Built It

Honestly? So I could keep making games and still be home for dinner.

Mad Tea Party is one person. Marketing well is a full-time job on its own, and I couldn't do it, keep building games and software, provide for my family, and still have time to be with them. So I built Looking Glass to carry the clipping and posting while I build.

It's a stand-in, not a replacement. As soon as I can afford to, I'll hire real people for these roles, and the day a role is filled, I stop using Looking Glass for that job in my own studio.

Questions

Is it a subscription?

No. One payment, yours to keep.

Does it post on its own?

Only what you set it to: approve each clip, or turn on automatic approval. Going-live posts use your own words.

Does it download my Twitch VODs?

Drop in your recording or VOD file and it clips it. Automatic Twitch downloading is an option you turn on yourself; it may break Twitch's terms.

Which streaming platforms?

Any, because it works from the recording file. Going-live detection is for Twitch.
