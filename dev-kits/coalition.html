The Dream

A whole Wonderland of studios where the players and the world are welcome guests, never the last thing on the menu.

Count hearts before coins

The coalition is a circle of indie developers who promote one another's games and hold themselves to a higher standard. Instead of elbowing for the spotlight, we pass it around the table, so every good game made with care gets its turn in the light.

Count hearts before coins, and the coins will find their way to the table.

The Criteria

Every studio at the table meets all four.

High-quality games

Made with care, finished with pride, and fun to play.

10%+

To a grassroots charity

At least a tenth of what every game earns goes to a grassroots charity.

A clean slate

No controversies in the last year.
